Compare competing next actions before declaring one primary.

This public-only CTA Choice Matrix helps an operator compare two visible calls to action, their immediate handoffs, and the visitor each one appears to serve. It is a writing and prioritization aid—not a traffic, conversion, or revenue estimate.

Public-only and observational. Work from visible page language and destinations only. Do not sign in, submit anything, use private analytics, test checkout, or infer customer behavior. Two actions can both be valid; the point is to make their roles legible before changing them.

Keep both only if the page can name the difference before a visitor chooses. A nearby qualifier can be enough.

Choose one plain label and subordinate the duplicate. Do not assume the duplicate creates an outcome problem.

Neither action has a legible handoff

Capture the exact cues and ask an owner which first decision matters before drafting a broader change.
