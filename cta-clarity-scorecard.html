Customer Capture Field Notes · printable field tool

Score one public CTA path before proposing a bigger change.

This paper-first six-point scorecard helps an operator check whether a visible promise, one intended action, and the immediate handoff fit together. It scores clarity cues—not traffic, conversion, revenue, or business quality.

Public-only and observational. Read only what any visitor can see. Do not sign in, submit forms, use private analytics, test checkout, or claim a business is losing money. A low score is a cue to ask an owner question, not proof of an outcome.

Score: ____ / 6
5–6: Preserve the strong cue

Record the exact words that already connect. Do not declare a performance result from the score.

3–4: Draft one narrow clarification

Try “[Verb] [specific outcome] — [what happens next]” and ask the owner if that is the intended path.

0–2: Ask before redesigning

Capture the mismatch and ask which visitor action matters. Do not infer a business problem or make a bigger claim.

Boundary: this scorecard is a public observation and writing aid. It does not collect data, submit anything, test accounts or checkout, assess security/accessibility/compliance, request private information, or establish business results.
